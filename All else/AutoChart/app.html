Auto Level Generator

Drop in a piece of music. It finds where the notes start, which notes and chords are playing, how long each lasts (so it can make holds), and which kind of instrument is playing, then lays out a RhythmDrop level whose notes are the song's own. Everything happens on this computer.

or drop it here. Classical music with clear notes works best.
